Scaffold's patterns 4 patterns · candle structure

Each one reads structure its own way.

PAT-01

Transit

The market stops persuading

Transit is a structural pause. A move in one direction gets met by a decisive response from the other side. Not just a reversal — the moment initiative changes hands.

Reversal ATR filter
PAT-02

Fulcrum

Build-up — then a response

Fulcrum is the moment initiative shifts to the other side. A pause, a hold — then a decision. Today this setup lives on as its own thing inside the experimental Sentry indicator, not here and not in Contour.

Reversal → Sentry
PAT-03

Needle

A sharp moment of refusal

Needle: the market tries to continue — and sharply refuses. Single, clean, easy to read. An optional confirmation waits for the next bar to close.

Reversal Confirm delay
PAT-04

Volatile Bar

Movement context

Flags any bar whose range exceeds the last N bars. Baseline context — Transit and Fulcrum are both built on top of volatile bars.
