How Penumbra works 6 pieces · from signal to zone

From the moment of absorption to tracking the zone on the chart.

FEAT-01◆

Absorption point

◆ marks the moment aggression gets absorbed in a candle's delta — before it's obvious on the chart. Works on both sides of the market.

◆ Bull / Bear Intrabar delta
FEAT-02⬜

Zone confirmation

Structural confirmation: ◆ doesn't turn into a composite Undertow signal without its own market footprint — a Fair Value Gap zone on the following bars.

FVG confirmation ◆ Undertow
FEAT-03⏱

Delayed confirmation

The signal doesn't rush: ◆ Undertow appears only once the market itself confirms the point with a zone — not sooner. It's not a delay, it's a condition.

Not instant Zone-gated
FEAT-04📐

Zone tracking

Every ◆ leaves a zone on the chart with two states: Active — the zone is untouched, Tested — price has traded back into it. A ▲/▼ marker appears on the test bar.

Active Tested
FEAT-05⚙️

Delta modes

Standard — higher precision from lower-timeframe data. Precision — real bid/ask data, maximum accuracy. Both modes require Premium.

Standard Precision Premium
FEAT-06⚖

Relative Strength

Compares the current instrument against a reference — NQ, ES, VIX, DXY. Optionally filters ◆ Undertow signals by relative strength over N bars.

NQ · ES · VIX · DXY
Premium

TradingView subscription requirement

Standard and Precision modes both require Premium. The indicator defaults to Standard. Premium is also needed for index data (GER40, S&P 500, etc.).
