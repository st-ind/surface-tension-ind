LEGACY-01

Penumbra

An early version of the signal layer

Finds hidden imbalance in a bar's intrabar delta and confirms it with its own Fair Value Gap zone on the following bars. That logic became the basis for the Undertow signal inside Meridian.

LEGACY-02

Scaffold

4 reversal patterns · any timeframe

Four independent candle-structure patterns: Transit, Fulcrum, Needle, and Volatile Bar. This logic became the starting point for Contour — the Fulcrum setup was later spun out of Contour into a separate experimental indicator, Sentry, while the underlying reversal-recognition idea now lives on inside Contour's 6-state machine.
