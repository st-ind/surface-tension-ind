Trading session schedule

Times are given in CET/CEST for trading the CME future from a European time zone.

14:00–15:30
Pre-market / overnight analysis
Assess ONH/ONL, the reaction to the European session, and the morning US data.
15:30–16:00
Period A — Open Type / OD
The RTH open. The first reaction to the overnight ONH/ONL levels.
16:00–16:30
Period B — Bias
The second IB block. A scored Bias forms here, and it almost always gets confirmed by a breakout.
16:30–17:00
Period C — THE MAIN WINDOW long short
IB is closed — the main window for entries. IB Extension plays out in almost every session — filter by Bias, not by the fact of the breakout.
17:00–18:00
Slower window
Manage the position, take partials, usually no new entries.
18:00–20:00
Afternoon
A quiet stretch of the day. Setups possible at VWAP tests and EQH/EQL magnets.
21:30–22:00
Evening surge — the second window
Before the exchange closes (~21:30) funds square positions — sharp moves, enter only with a clear daily context.
Overnight (ON)
Trading before the official session open — before 15:30 Berlin time. While the "main" day hasn't started yet, price still moves: overnight and in the morning it sets an overnight high and low (often written ONH/ONL). When the session opens at 15:30, the market often reacts to those first — not only to yesterday's levels.
03 / differences

Key differences from FDAX

The methodology is the same, but the risk parameters and IB-breakout expectations need to be recalculated for NQ.

MetricNQFDAX

Neutral Dayrarehappens more often

Company earningsheavily — Nvidia, Apple, Microsoftbarely a factor

Day's open (RTH)
The "real" start of trading — from 15:30 Berlin time, when the American market opens. In Relief these are periods A–Z. Everything before 15:30 is the overnight session.
Company earnings
NQ is a tech-giant index. When Nvidia, Apple, or Microsoft report quarterly results, price can whip around outside the profile's usual logic. Cut position size or skip entries during those weeks.
Evening surge (MOC)
Around 21:30–22:00, before the exchange closes, large funds square positions "on the close." The result is a sharp one-sided flow — a second volatility "window" at the end of the day. FDAX has nothing like it.
04 / prep

Pre-open prep (14:00–15:29)

Overnight and in the morning before 15:30 (overnight), price often already covers most of the day's range — which is why overnight context matters more on NQ than on FDAX.

🧭

Context

  • Where is price relative to PDVAH/PDVAL? Outside Above / Inside / Outside Below
  • Is there a Naked POC nearby? → a magnet, don't trade against it
  • What kind of day was yesterday — Poor H/L? A return to it today is likely
🌙

Overnight levels

  • ONH/ONL — the overnight high and low, the main reference points at the 15:30 open. On NQ, watch them separately from GER40.
  • A large gap at the open → an Open Drive is more likely
📰

News

  • 08:30 ET data (CPI, NFP, jobless claims) — stay out 15 minutes before / 5 minutes after
  • 14:00 ET (FOMC) — same rule
  • Earnings weeks (January / April / July / October) — cut position size
05 / period A

Period A (15:30–16:00) — read the open

The RTH open reacts first to the overnight ONH/ONL levels, not only to yesterday's Value Area.

↑ OD Long

  • All of period A stays above the open, no TPO blocks against the move → Trend Day up
  • Entry: the first pullback into period A's lower TPO, 38.2–50% of the IB range deep
  • Stop: beyond period A's high/low
  • Target 1: PDPOC / Naked POC · Target 2: PDVAH in the direction of the move

↓ OD Short

  • All of period A stays below the open, no TPO blocks against the move → Trend Day down
  • Entry: the first pullback into period A's upper TPO, 38.2–50% of the IB range deep
  • Stop: beyond period A's high/low
  • Target 1: PDPOC / Naked POC · Target 2: PDVAL in the direction of the move
06 / period B

Period B (16:00–16:30) — Bias forms

On NQ, Bias almost always gets confirmed by an IB break — the question isn't whether it breaks, only which way. Relief scores ▲ L n/5 / ▼ S n/5: the VWAP side, A's direction, B's arrow, B breaking A's range, close relative to the IB Mid. The same lean shows up as Sig Bias in the info panel.

ScoreMeaningAction
4–5 / 5Strong narrativeTrade continuation in the direction of Bias — entries that way are priority
3 / 5Working thresholdThe minimum for an entry — trade it, but cut size
0–2 / 5No narrativeThe lean is weak — wait for a cleaner setup
What Bias means for the entry in C

▲ Bias UP

  • Long setups on the IBH break — the priority direction
  • A Raid G/S in the long direction — a valid entry signal
  • FA↓ (a failed IBL break) confirms Bias → enter long

▼ Bias DOWN

  • Short setups on the IBL break — the priority direction
  • A Raid G/S in the short direction — a valid entry signal
  • FA↑ (a failed IBH break) confirms Bias → enter short
07 / period C

Period C (16:30–17:00) — the main window

The same three setups as FDAX, but with NQ-sized risk parameters — wider stops because of the noise, a different pullback depth.

Setup 1 · IB Extension

96% of days
  1. IB is closed, Bias ≥ 3/5
  2. The first TPO closed beyond IB High (↑) or IB Low (↓)
  3. Entry: a pullback 38.2–50% deep into the IB range — ideally with a ◆ + FVG signal in the direction of the break (see section 8)
  4. Stop: a return inside IB > 15 points (accepting back inside)
  5. Targets: Relief's 0.5× → 1× IB levels from the break edge (reached ones get a ✓)
  6. Target 2: 1.5–2× IB — on a confirmed Trend Day
  7. Filter: the break alone isn't a signal — on NQ it's almost guaranteed, Bias is what matters
  8. Be careful with SW ↓/↑ at IBH/IBL — a first break that sweeps and returns is a sweep, not acceptance

Setup 2 · Confirmed raid

Raid G / S
  1. Wait for a Raid G/S signal (confirmed, not the live version)
  2. The raid's direction matches Bias from B
  3. Entry on the pullback after confirmation
  4. Stop: 12–15 points beyond the raid level — NQ carries more noise than FDAX
  5. Target: Naked POC → PDPOC → the opposite edge of the VA

Setup 3 · Failed Auction

FA
  1. An FA signal appeared (IB was broken, but price returned inside)
  2. Bias from B is against the break (confirming the FA)
  3. Entry back inside IB, in the direction of Bias
  4. Stop: 15 points beyond the IB edge
  5. Target: IB Mid → Naked POC → PDPOC
08 / entry confirmation

◆ + FVG — the entry trigger

Setups A–C give you context. The ◆ + FVG signal in Relief is the specific bar to enter on, once the day's picture is already in place. The alert fires on bar close.

◆ + FVG

Alert
  1. Context is ready: period C, IB closed, Bias ≥ 3/5
  2. A period-C setup is already on the chart — ◆ + FVG confirms the entry, it doesn't replace the setup
  3. Only in the direction of Sig Bias — Relief won't show a signal against the day's direction
  4. Entry: close of the signal bar or a pullback into the zone on the chart
  5. Stop: beyond the extreme of the ◆ bar — leave extra room for NQ's noise
  6. Targets: Naked POC, IB Mid, IB extensions (0.5× → 1× → 1.5×)
  7. Price broke through the zone and held beyond it — the idea is invalidated, don't average in
09 / management

Position management

Relief's magnet levels for partial profit-taking, and the forced exit signals.

🔵

nPOC (Naked)

The first partial take-profit. A strong magnet from untested prior days.

🟡

VWAP

The first stop in the direction of the trade. The session's first test (★) matters most.

🧲

EQH/EQL + magnet

A cluster of stop-hunt levels. A target for the second partial (TP2).

🔁

Poor H/L

An unfinished auction from prior days — the market comes back to close it within 1–3 days.

Forced exits
  • A raid signal against your position — exit immediately, don't wait for confirmation
  • An HL Sweep run against you — liquidity being taken your way, close the position
  • 3+ consecutive TPO accumulation periods — rare on NQ, but if it happens the move has run out of steam
10 / MOC window

Evening surge (21:30–22:00)

Before the exchange closes, large players square positions — producing a sharp one-sided flow. It's not a repeat of the morning session; it behaves differently.

  • Enter only with a clear daily context — a Trend Day with a strong Bias from early in the session
  • Check the current Value Area and price's position relative to VWAP before entering
  • The trigger is a ◆ + FVG signal in the direction of Sig Bias, once the daily context is already clear
  • Moves are sharper and faster than the morning — keep the stop wider than usual
  • Without clear context — better to skip the window than guess the direction of the flow
11 / day types

Day types and tactics

The Day Type label in Relief's info panel: Trend ↑/↓, NV ↑/↓ (Normal Variation), Normal, Neutral, Tight, ORR. On NQ, Neutral Day is rare: an IB break happens in 96.2% of sessions, so the real question isn't "will it break" but "which way."

Trend ↑/↓

Trend Day

Signs

Narrow IB + Open Drive + Bias 4–5/5 + Single Print accumulation

Tactics

Enter on pullbacks, don't fade the move, trail the stop behind periods

Normal

Normal Day

Signs

One IB break, a moderate Bias — the most common day type on NQ

Tactics

The IB Extension setup, partial off at 1× IB

Neutral

Neutral Day

Signs

Both edges of IB break — rare on NQ (unlike FDAX)

Tactics

Fade the second break, target the IB Mid

ORR

Open-Rejection-Reverse

Signs

Open outside the VA + a quick return inside

Tactics

Enter on the retest of the VA edge from inside, target the PDPOC

12 / stop list

When NOT to trade

⚠️ Red flags

  • 15 minutes before / 5 minutes after 08:30 ET — CPI, NFP, jobless claims data
  • 14:00 ET — FOMC meetings, rate decision releases
  • NVDA / AAPL / MSFT / META earnings day and the day after — a distorted profile
  • The MOC window (21:30–22:00 CEST) — only with a clear daily context, otherwise skip it
  • IB > 150 points — the trend is already running without you, the moment is gone
