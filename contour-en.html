Architecture 4 layers · L1 → L4

From a closed period to an entry signal — one pass, no separate indicators stacked on top.

Layer 1

Period → metrics

At the close of a 30-minute period: OTF extremes against N−1, a Rotation Factor (−2…+2), range-overlap percentage, a POC shift (hl2/close proxy), and tails at a new session extreme.

Layer 2

6-state machine

Top-down classification, first matching rule wins: Strong Trend → Pullback → Fading → Reversal → Sweep → Balance. Reversal is confirmed only by the next period — otherwise it's demoted to Sweep.

Layer 3

Status table + plot series

An optional State / Direction / Reason table in the chart corner. Separately, 4 series are always computed for your own strategies: state, allowed direction, invalidation level, running daily RF.

Layer 4

Δ+FVG signal

A delta bar plus a Fair Value Gap, filtered by the period's read — with additive VWAP / EQ / Volatile Bar / level bypasses and transparent tags right on the signal badge.

States 6 auction reads · + 1 no-read

Every closed period is checked top-down against six rules — the first match wins. Strong Trend is confirmed immediately; the other five are confirmed only once period N+1 fails to contradict the read.

1 Strong Trend OTF streak ≥2, |RF|=2, extension holds, pullback <50%. "OTF×3 RF+2 ext held"
→
2 Pullback Body holds inside a Single Print zone, 30–60% retrace, no break. "pullback 42% SP hold"
→
3 Fading 2+ of 5 exhaustion signs: decay, growing overlap, a tail, POC not following, RF dropped. "fade vote 3/5"
→
4 Reversal A close breaks the old extreme AFTER fading — a confirmed flip in side. "OTF break after fade, RF+1"
→
5 Sweep A break with no prior fade — or a wick pierces the level and closes back. "wick pierce, closed back"
→
6 Balance Two periods in a row overlap ≥70% of range, |RF|≤1 — no edge for either side. "overlap 78% ×2"
0 · Undefined — none of the six rules matched. The period is left with no read: no allowed direction, no invalidation level.

Reversal is the only read that can walk itself back: if period N+1 closes back past the break level, the read is retroactively demoted to Sweep. An unconfirmed reversal stays unconfirmed — no exceptions.

Signals Δ+FVG · a transparent badge

The badge doesn't just say "this signal passed" — it shows why. Every bypass that passed gets its own dot-separated tag next to the direction.

↑ long ↓ short orange = inside a BR ✓ window

Badge anatomy

The arrow is the direction of the delta bar with a confirmed FVG. Passed bypasses then get glued on with a dot: ↑·F, ↓·Eq, ↑·V·PD. One signal can carry several tags at once — every reason it passed the filter is right there.

F

After a VWAP break

The first signal in either direction after N confirming closes past VWAP. The signal's direction must match the break direction — otherwise no tag gets attached, and the arm just resets silently.

V

Volatile Bar window

A signal in the direction of a colored (non-gray) VB dot, within an N-bar window starting at the VB bar itself. Also fires when only the signal's delta bar landed in the window and the FVG completed later.

Eq

EQ liquidity

The signal's own delta bar formed a significant equal high/low — but the tag only attaches if that agrees with the currently forming period's color.

FR · LDN

Session level sweep

Sweeping the Frankfurt/London high arms shorts, sweeping the low arms longs (mean-reversion). Stays armed with no time limit until the next signal that actually passes.

PD · PW

Day/week level sweep

Same logic for pdH/pdL and pwH/pwL: sweeping the high arms shorts, the low arms longs. Tracked independently of whether the level lines are even shown on the chart.

RS

Re-sweep

Any FR/LDN/PD/PW-tagged signal leaves a level behind at its own delta bar. If price sweeps that level again, the next signal in the SAME direction gets an RS tag: a second liquidity grab on top of the first.

br → BR ✓ → BR ▲

The CHoCH label's evolution

A break against the previous break starts as "br". A close past the level with the candle BODY upgrades it to "BR ✓". If a BOS in that new direction arrives before price closes back — "BR ▲"/"BR ▼", a confirmed trend change. Live demo: br

orange ◆

BR ✓ window highlight

On a body-close CHoCH, every already-displayed signal of the same direction inside the window "broken pivot − N bars → break bar" gets repainted orange — retroactively showing which ◆ signals led into that break.

▲ / ▼

FVG test

The first touch of a Fair Value Gap zone after it forms gets marked with a directional triangle — no strong/weak grading, just the fact of a first test.

alert()

Alerts

3 static conditions (either direction / Long / Short) plus a dynamic alert() with the ticker and timeframe right in the message text — for every signal that actually passes, not the filtered-out ones.
