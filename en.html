FAQ Frequently asked

Getting started

What timeframe do I need, and why 1m?+

Relief only runs on the 1-minute chart. TPO periods last 30 minutes, and the profile needs 1m candles to build accurately — on higher timeframes the cubes come out wrong or don't appear at all. Check the "Session start" setting matches your market's open (e.g. 8:00 CET for GER40).

What's "Ticks per row"?+

The size of one TPO row in ticks. It has to match the "Ticks per row" setting in TradingView's own TPO indicator, or the profiles won't line up.

Do I need TradingView Premium?+

Yes, for some features. Index data (GER40, S&P 500, etc.) is Premium+ only. Meridian's Undertow Precision mode uses internal TradingView APIs for lower timeframes and bid/ask, which also needs Premium or above. Relief and Contour's core TPO profile work on any plan — the free plan's real limit is 1m history depth (~1,500 bars).

How do I get access to the indicators?+

The indicators are closed-source/invite-only. Get in touch with the author after picking a plan.

Levels & zones

What are Poor High / Poor Low?+

A session extreme touched by fewer than two TPO periods — a sign of an unfinished auction. These zones often get revisited, so treat them as a target or a reversal zone.

What's nPOC, and what do its states mean?+

The previous day's POC, still untouched by price — a strong magnet. Blue: untouched. Red: wick-tested, still active. Grey: closed through by a candle body, frozen at the break. History depth is configurable (20 days by default, 60 max).

How do I read pVAH/pVAL and open type?+

pVAH and pVAL are yesterday's Value Area bounds (68% of volume). Opening above pVAH is a bullish context (Outside above), below pVAL is bearish (Outside below), and opening inside the VA means a two-sided auction — trade with more caution.

What are EQH/EQL and the magnet mark?+

Equal Highs/Lows — levels touched more than once without breaking, with stops stacked behind them. When several EQ levels cluster together, the indicator marks a magnet — a zone of elevated pull on price.

VWAP & signals

What does the ★ mean on the Relief chart?+

The session's first wick test of VWAP after period B — the most meaningful one, since it's the market's first return to session VWAP after bias has formed. Every other test gets a plain circle.

What's the difference between Raid G and Raid S?+

Both are liquidity grabs off the previous period's extremes. Raid G (Grab) — price pushed through and snapped back fast: a false break, likely reversal. Raid S (Sweep) — price stayed past the level for N bars: possible continuation.

What does HL Sweep mean?+

It appears after a run of N periods in the same direction, when the next period breaks the last extreme and snaps back quickly. The number in the mark is the run length — the longer it is, the more it signals trend exhaustion.

How does Bias work on period B?+

At the close of period B, Relief scores a ▲L n/5 or ▼S n/5 read: close position relative to VWAP, plus up to 4 more points for A/B direction, an IBH/IBL break, and position versus IB Mid. It's a standalone context mark, not part of the info panel.

More

Do the indicators work on instruments other than GER40?+

Yes, on any TradingView instrument. Tune it to your market: session start (hour/minute CET), Ticks per row (match TradingView's own TPO), and VA % (usually 68%).

How much history is kept?+

nPOC goes back up to 60 days (configurable). The real limit is TradingView's own 1m data depth — 1,500–5,000 bars on the free plan. If early periods aren't showing, that's a platform limit, not the indicator.

Why do some signals disappear after a few candles?+

That's Raid confirmation: the indicator waits N candles (3 by default) after a Grab/Sweep. If price hasn't reversed by then, the signal is marked false and removed. Set the confirmation parameter to 0 to disable it.

Can I trade off a single signal?+

Technically, yes — but without context (open type, Bias, VWAP) it performs noticeably worse. The strongest setups are where several signals line up, e.g. a Raid G in the direction of Bias at pVAH on an Outside open.
