◈ Relief — Signal Library

Six ways the market reveals its hand.

🎣

Raid G / Raid S

Grab = false break, quick return. Sweep = extremum held N bars, possible continuation.

📉

HL Sweep Series

A run of same-direction periods that finally sweeps and reverses — trend exhaustion.

🚀

Open Drive ↑/↓

One-directional push straight from the open — no rotation, high conviction.

🕳️

Poor H/L

Fewer than two TPOs at the extreme — an unfinished auction the market tends to revisit.

🧲

nPOC Magnet

Untouched prior-day POC — a gravity level tracked forward until price fills it.

⚠️

Failed Auction

Attempted breakout rejected back into range — early warning of a trapped move.
