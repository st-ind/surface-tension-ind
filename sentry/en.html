FAQ Frequently asked questions
Do I need TradingView Premium?+

No. Sentry calculates everything on the current chart and doesn't use any Premium-only features. It works on any plan, including the free one.

Which timeframes and instruments does Sentry work on?+

Any of them. All patterns are calculated bar-by-bar on the current chart, and previous day/week levels update automatically by calendar. Tune the zigzag sensitivity and setup parameters to fit your instrument and timeframe.

What's the difference between an orange and a gray Volatile Bar dot?+

A gray dot is a plain volatile bar (its range exceeds the previous N bars). Orange is the same bar, but it's also the first to sweep a zigzag high/low or a pdH/pdL/pwH/pwL level. A swept zigzag point additionally gets a dashed line running to the bar where it was swept.

Does Sentry have BOS/CHoCH like other indicators?+

No — in Sentry, the zigzag is only used as a source of swing points for Volatile Bar Sweep detection and as a standalone structure visualization. Break of structure (BOS/CHoCH) isn't tracked in the current version — the focus here is on Volatile Bar Sweep.

How do I get access to the indicator?+

The indicator is invite-only. To get access, contact the author after paying for your chosen plan.
